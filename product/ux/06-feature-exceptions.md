# Feature exceptions

These are the justified deviations from ordinary authenticated editing. They preserve the shared visual language. They do not create new product authority.

| Rule | Scope and reason | Required experience and fallback | Conformance effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UX-EX01 | Audit unavailable; AUDIT-04 / SECRES-04 | Persistent **Read-only: audit recording unavailable** banner. Preserve only role-scoped reads and diagnosis; sensitive actions, exports, backups and recovery mutations are unavailable with the cause stated. Restricted new login is offered only when reliable verification/replay/throttling exist; otherwise give bounded failure and host-diagnostic guidance. Host storage/process repair is the recovery route, never an unaudited web bypass. Local logout remains available. After repair, a restricted diagnostic session must sign in normally again. | Hidden or disabled mutation controls never suggest stale action success; health repair does not silently upgrade a restricted session. |
| UX-EX02 | Bootstrap, invitations, capability recovery and restore enrollment; SECRES-01–03 | Restricted pages show only the bound identity, operation, safe status and completion/cancel or recovery guidance. No ordinary shell grants or service links before enrollment/audit prerequisites. Expired or lost proof routes to authorized reissue/host recovery. Opening a link never consumes its final operation. Fresh factors display only in the protected enrollment step. | Shared form/accessibility rules apply; restricted authority never looks like a fully active account. |
| UX-EX03 | Immediate access, credential and lifecycle changes; STATE-01/03 | Separate explicit confirmation and immediate result; do not put secret-value rotation, disable/revoke or account recovery behind Save draft. Initial service activation may explicitly include newly reviewed assignments. History opens configuration only as a new draft. | Author cannot infer that discard/history reverses an immediate action. |
| UX-EX04 | Restore installation and management review; STATE-05/06 | Full-page maintenance/status until installation outcome is known. Show actual initiator and nominated recovery Owner distinctly. Failed rollback exposes host recovery; clean-host failure remains restricted. Fresh Owner enrollment then opens management review with disabled services/pending users, explicit per-service activation and client reconnection. | No fake rollback guarantee, bulk resume or stale-auth continuation; imported evidence provenance remains visible. |
| UX-EX05 | Advanced external OAuth and legacy bearer; MCP-02 | Owner-only advanced configuration with clear no-console-access/shared-attribution explanations, explicit identity registration/assignments and reactivation after restore. Normal client setup foregrounds built-in per-user OAuth. | Do not present legacy/shared attribution as equivalent to individual MFA-protected console identity. |

Other features inherit the shared UX rules. The public health view exposes only bounded healthy/degraded status; no detailed diagnostics, account enumeration or bootstrap discovery is implied by the richer authenticated health views.
