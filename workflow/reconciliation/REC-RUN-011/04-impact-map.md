## Shared-pattern scope and preservation

- Validation: imported/new/existing guided services, service overview readiness, Settings field errors. Existing linked local-error contract governs all; audit directly observed only the missing-service-value case. Include equivalent supported malformed destinations/placements/policy and missing mappings in implementation acceptance.
- Progression: new and existing/imported guided forms share the four-section rule; direct section links, Advanced mode, Back/Forward and unsaved guards remain.
- Decision visibility: service exceptions are conforming evidence; Settings effect summaries, health details and retention receive exact settled changes. This does not globalize the service-specific approval modal to Settings.
- Credentials: service dialogs are a conforming sibling for focus, blank write-only value and Cancel. SMTP preserves its justified UX-EX03 timing exception: Set/Replace stages review/apply, Clear uses independent dependency-checked immediate deletion.
- Settings organization/search: bounded design interpretation only; no blanket unfolding of advanced editors or loss of existing options. Any selected design must apply consistently to writable and authorized read-only siblings.
- Export/history: underlying exporter sanitization and history authority remain; change presentation and download only. No raw secret backup or access replay.
