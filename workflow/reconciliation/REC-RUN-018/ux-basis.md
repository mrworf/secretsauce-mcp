# UX13 bounded shared-pattern resolution

Proposed Product UX input to REC018; not canonical authority or runtime acceptance. Confirmed PO-FB299–350 supplies the direction. Existing UX12 appearance, service/reference/access boundaries and SMTP mutation timing remain. No new consequential UX choice or concept approval is required.

## U13-01 — Clean editors and consistent read-only configuration

Hide the shared Discard / Save for later / Review changes bar only when there are neither actual browser-unsaved changes nor saved pending proposals in its authorized scope. A saved draft remains a change awaiting Apply even when the local form is clean. Read-only views have no mutation bar. Hidden bars relinquish their reserved space and cannot intercept focus; showing them preserves focused-control and full-label clearance. Apply across service Guided/Advanced/initial Access and all editable Settings forms; current applied Access remains its independent immediate task. Genuine errors/unknown state must remain visible independently of the bar.

Archived Configuration uses the same sections, field order, labels, cards, spacing, value summaries and width as Active/Inactive Configuration, with read-only semantics and no configuration mutation controls. Preserve copy/select/read access, exact Advanced representation where permitted, missing-value status without revealing values, and accessible navigation. Existing separate lifecycle/permanent-delete/saved-discard actions retain their permissions; making Configuration read-only does not remove authorized lifecycle management. Do not merely gray out an unrelated summary page.

Remove standalone Required legends across equivalent console forms/dialogs, retaining field-level asterisks, semantic required state, local labels and conditional rules. Read-only text does not become an invalid required form. Keep substantive help and field errors. Access rows need no repeated Explicitly assigned qualifier: show person, role and ordinary assignment/action state. Short contextual help explains that being listed grants use subject to current eligibility and policy; Owner/Administrator status alone grants no service use. This resolves the question without inventing inherited/group access.

## U13-02 — One routine success feedback pattern

Routine confirmed save/apply/grant/remove/account operations use a compact dismissible nonmodal success notification in the upper-right workspace below the top navigation. Keep sidebar and primary controls unobscured; reserve an appropriate notification area or reflow at narrow widths rather than overlaying controls or form errors. Announce politely through an already-present status region without moving focus. Use concise task-specific text, deduplicate/sequence bursts rather than stacking an unlimited column, and retain established visual styling.

Use about five seconds for ordinary automatic dismissal, paused while hovered or keyboard-focused; the Close control works without a task deadline. Auto-expiry is permitted only when equivalent result information remains available without a time limit in the refreshed object/status or durable authorized activity. Otherwise keep the information persistent. No new notification-center subsystem is needed. Validation errors, security warnings, failed/unknown mutations and post-Apply cleanup failures remain persistent actionable contextual messages until resolved or deliberately dismissed where safe; never convert them to disappearing success toasts. Initial loading, pending operation and independent delivery failures must not be reported as success.

Downloads need no success toast: specifically remove the Access inventory confirmation, and apply the same rule to equivalent successful exports. Keep download failure/unknown-outcome feedback and audit requirements. Rename the people/access action **Export service assignments**. Do not remove required pre-download safeguards or claim browser file persistence was verified.

Research basis (accessed2026-11-20): [Carbon notification guidelines](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/notification/guidelines) distinguish short top-right nonmodal success feedback from persistent contextual/actionable messages and recommend an alternative way to revisit expiring information. [WCAG timing guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable) permits transient status when the same information remains available independently; a timeout must not remove the sole opportunity to act. [ARIA22](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/aria/ARIA22.html) supports polite status announcements without focus movement. Placement, five-second default, pause and no-toast-for-download are bounded design application of the confirmed preference, not claims that WCAG mandates a particular toast style.

## U13-03 — Local dates, explicit ranges and honest Activity state

Display timestamps consistently in the browser's local time zone and identify that zone near the relevant group or timestamp; use precise offsets where needed to distinguish repeated daylight-saving times. Internal storage/API instants remain UTC. Apply to Activity filter inputs/results and equivalent account, credential, backup, health and history timestamps. Fixed operational schedules explicitly defined in UTC, such as daily backup hour, remain labeled UTC schedules, not silently converted stored instructions. Machine-readable exports retain unambiguous UTC instants.

Activity initially selects **Last 24 hours**, with its initial query resolving a concrete interval. **All time** and **Custom** are separate choices: All time disables From/To and imposes no date bounds; Custom enables them with precise validation. Presets display their resolved local bounds and do not silently turn into All time. Changing any filter/chip/range/custom bound clears rows, previous result counts, pagination and stale export authority immediately; show **Filters changed. Select Search to see results.** Do not auto-search on edits or Clear. Initial entry may load its default query once. Preserve fixed nonremovable per-service context and Member authorization. Canceling an uncommitted filter dialog does not change criteria and therefore retains the current results. A deliberate Search/Refresh resolves relative presets anew; pagination does not. Returning through history may restore criteria, but must not display results or enable export from a different query; require Search when a matching successful result context is not retained. No navigation or filter edit triggers an implicit replacement search.

Search validates inputs and captures exact criteria plus absolute interval for the result set. Pagination continues that searched interval, never recomputes a moving relative preset or drops chips. Show **1–50 of 135 events**, **Page 1 of 3**, Previous/Next and a bounded page-size selector beside paging; use existing valid default size, with25/50/100 choices within the existing100 maximum. Zero results show0 events without fictitious ranges/pages. Page-size change is presentation paging on the same searched criteria, resets to page1 and may fetch that page; it is not a filter edit. Pending/failed responses cannot resurrect results after newer filter edits or navigation.

Label **Export filtered activity**. It uses the successful searched criteria and absolute range, current authorization and fixed context, exporting all matching retained events independently of visible page/size. Disable until a current successful Search after filter edits; no stale-count or page-offset implication. Counts describe the current returned search snapshot; retention/new events may change the independently admitted export snapshot, so do not promise exact historical row-count identity. Existing resource limits cannot silently truncate an advertised complete download; bounded export delivery semantics are reconciler/domain-owned. No download toast.

Keep applied audit-retention duration visible and current after Apply. Remove Displayed search debug text, standing protected-checkpoint explanation and blanket uncertain-outcome instruction. Actual integrity failures and specific uncertain operations retain actionable status. Documentation may explain integrity separately; this does not weaken enforcement. Activity shows only live current-host sources. Restore must not install archived Activity records as queryable sources; this is removal of the import behavior, not merely a visual filter. Existing legacy evidence domains are not purged by this UI change; historical backup/export content may remain under its retention rules. Do not expose a general evidence upload task.

## U13-04 — Settings review as a focused complete-scope task

Opening/reading a populated form or rendering equivalent units/formatting must not create edits or phantom saved proposals. Dirty checks compare domain values with their baseline while preserving exact significant literals, regex/options/array order and write-only credential semantics. Comparison renders equivalent current/proposed values using the same units and layout; it does not normalize stored values merely for display. Retention-only edits must not save untouched Secret Detection or fabricate its differences. All real authorized stored drafts remain in complete-scope review; do not skip/discard unrelated pending work to hide errors. Rebase only a proven unchanged proposal baseline with fresh dependencies; otherwise retain work and identify the actual section needing repair.

Settings review is a dedicated task in the existing workspace with the Settings subsection rail hidden and main navigation retained. Preserve grouped current/proposed comparison and the complete authorized review scope. Remove the generic Security and operational effects box. Concrete changed-setting risks still appear where relevant and mandatory acknowledgments remain enforced; removing generic prose is not removal of security/retention effects.

Reuse Services **Approve changes** confirmation: review page action opens a focused modal describing Apply, with Continue/Cancel and each required acknowledgment clearly visible. Continue is disabled until all required checks are satisfied; bind current reviewed scope/dependencies and preserve existing full-scope acknowledgment and unencrypted-SMTP requirements. No checkbox remains obscured behind a fixed page action. Confirmation does not substitute for server validation. Cancel returns to the unchanged review and safe focus.

Capture the originating Settings section when review starts. On confirmed successful Apply, refresh current state and return there with the shared concise success notification, not a separate success screen. Partial save/Apply failure or unknown outcome retains safe work and explicit recovery; do not navigate as though success. Post-commit retention cleanup failure is a distinct persistent result, not a rollback. Review/back/history remain guarded. Discard saved changes lists affected sections as bullets and separately explains that settings in use remain unchanged; it does not undo independent immediate operations.
